What is Crossfire?

Crossfire is a unified game launcher. Bring together libraries from Steam, Xbox, and other platforms so you can browse, filter, and launch games from one place instead of hopping between apps.

What it does

  • Connect Steam, Xbox, and other game libraries in one panel
  • Browse your full collection without opening each store app
  • Launch games straight from Crossfire
  • Filter by platform, play status, or genre
  • Keep friends activity and recent games visible at a glance

How it works

Link libraries

Connect your Steam, Xbox, and other accounts so Play can see what you own.

Browse everything

Your games show up in one library — search, sort, and pin favorites.

Launch

Start a game from Play; it hands off to the right platform client when needed.

Features

Multi-platform

Steam, Xbox, and more — one launcher surface.

Unified library

See every game you own without juggling clients.

Quick launch

Start titles from a single list, not five different apps.

Clean control

Filters and favorites keep a big library easy to use.

Lightweight

Uses less than 100MB of memory.
