What is Mokio Arc?

Mokio Arc is an open-design messaging app made for easy communication. It starts as a fast, modern chat app, then opens up to mods and games so conversations can turn into play without switching platforms.

What it does

  • Direct messages, group chats, and calling with a simple, fast layout
  • Launch and play games straight from a conversation through the Mokio Panel
  • Find and join friends’ game sessions effortlessly
  • Link with Mokio Wave so you can listen to songs together

How it works

Start chatting

Arc launches as a simple app, with a web/app version connected through the Mokio interface.

Add a mod

Install a mod to use Arc as an overlay inside your favorite games or use the Mokio Panel.

Play together

Download and launch games through the Mokio Panel, and invite your friends.

Stay connected

Your account links to Wave and other Mokio products.
