What is Mokio Wave?

Mokio Wave looks like a regular music app, but it is so much more. It is built for listening and creating in the same place, with editing tools, a clean interface, and features that keep people connected around what they play.

What it does

  • Play and discover music with curated playlists and discovery tools
  • Cut and clip your favorite tracks with the built-in editing panel
  • Make your own music with Wave Studio
  • Save creations locally to your private library or publish them for the world to hear
  • Let friends follow what you play and listen together

How it works

Listen

Browse curated playlists and discovery tools to find new music and keep your library organized.

Create

Open the built-in editing panel to cut and clip tracks. No separate studio app needed.

Share

Save to your private library or publish directly so others can hear it.

Connect

Follow friends, drop into listening sessions, and keep listening social.

Features

Creator-friendly tools

Editing software is part of the app. Make tracks, trim, arrange, and share without leaving Mokio.

Social listening

Follow what friends are playing and drop into sessions together.

Discovery & playlists

Curated playlists and discovery tools help you find new music.

Clean interface

The player and studio share one visual language, so listening and making feel like one product.
